Results

A few projects I led, with the numbers before and after.

Listing and PPC overhaul for a best-selling kitchen product

Sales had been flat for three months, conversion was well below competitors, and almost a third of ad spend went to search terms that never sold.

I rebuilt the keywords and copy, briefed new images and A+ Content around customer complaints, moved the top converting terms into exact match and added 140 negative keywords.

Within 8 weeks, monthly sales grew from $18,000 to $29,000 with the same ad budget.

BeforeAfter
Conversion rate 8% to 13%
Monthly sales $18k to $29k
ACoS (lower is better) 42% to 24%
TACoS (lower is better) 19% to 11%

Review analysis that fixed a conversion problem

I analyzed about 1,200 reviews from our listing and three competitors and found that 18% of negative reviews in the category complained the product was hard to clean. Our listing never mentioned it.

I rewrote the bullets and briefed new images around the easy-clean feature, then localized the update for the UK and Germany. A process that used to take a week took one day.

BeforeAfter
Conversion rate 11% to 14%
Time to analyze and update ~7 days to 1 day
